About GAMBIT Lab

GAMBIT Lab — Game-theoretic AI, Mechanisms, Behavior, Incentives & Trustworthiness — is a research group in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at IIT Hyderabad, led by Dr. Ganesh Ghalme. We work at the intersection of game theory, mechanism design and machine learning, with a focus on building learning systems that remain fair, robust and well-behaved when deployed among strategic agents, including applications in AI for finance.
